Privacy Policy for AI Agent

Last updated: October 13, 2026

AI Agent is a personal assistant application. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information You Provide

When you send messages or use voice input, the information you provide may be processed to generate responses and operate app features.

Data Storage

The app may store certain information, such as preferences or details you ask it to remember, on the device or on a backend server configured by the app operator.

AI Services

If the app uses a third-party AI service, your submitted text or other input may be sent to that service to generate a response and will be handled under that provider's policies.

Permissions

The app may request microphone permission for voice input and internet access for online features. Device-control features may require additional Android permissions that you choose to enable.

Sharing and Security

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app. Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

Your Choices

You can stop using voice or online features and revoke app permissions through Android settings. Stored data may need to be deleted from the device or configured server separately.
